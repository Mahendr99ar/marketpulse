# Brief rating rubric

Rate each brief in `reports/<dataset>/briefs_<writer>.jsonl` by filling `rating_1_to_5`. Rate before you look at the fact-check result, and mix briefs from different writers so you do not know which wrote which.

Score each criterion 1 to 5, then record the average.

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy of claims | A wrong cause or a misleading statement | Minor overstatement | Every claim supported by the facts or headlines |
| Priority | Leads with a minor move | Main move present but buried | Biggest unusual move first |
| Driver | Invents a cause | Vague cause | Names the headline cause, or says none is clear |
| Usefulness | A trader would ignore it | Readable but generic | A salesperson could forward it with no edits |
| Length and style | Rambling or list-like | Some filler | 120 to 180 words, plain sentences |

Also note any error the fact-checker missed. Those notes are the best evidence of the checker's limits.
